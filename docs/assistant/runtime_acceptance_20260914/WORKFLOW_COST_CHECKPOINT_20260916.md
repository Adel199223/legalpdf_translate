## Time and remaining comparison requirements

The pilot's operation times include local work and are not pure provider latency. Reservation start-to-settlement intervals exclude later bilingual review, repairs and visual acceptance. Development-suite runtimes are also not document-delivery latency. No complete reviewed-delivery duration or 20% time saving is established.

A defensible later comparison must bind the same source set and accepted quality/layout requirements, count every OCR/translation/review/correction/retry call, identify cache treatment, and measure the complete delivery interval. A 20% cost target requires candidate cost at most 0.8 times the matched baseline. No additional paid comparison is justified merely to fill an incomplete denominator.

Terra High remains provisional. Extra High remains eligible when measured quality benefit warrants total workflow cost; selective Sol evaluation requires a concrete unresolved case. This checkpoint changes no model, reasoning-effort, protocol or saved formatting default.
