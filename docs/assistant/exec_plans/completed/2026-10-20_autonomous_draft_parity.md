## Goal and non-goals

Make the ordinary app produce source-faithful, readable Arabic translations comparable to the latest reviewed drafts without assistant edits to the resulting documents. Work sequentially through the latest three translated documents, correcting general product defects and rerunning each before proceeding. Evaluate meaning, completeness, protected facts and rendered layout rather than demanding identical prose or word counts.

Routine implementation and testing continue autonomously under this instruction rather than requesting old continuation tokens. ## Scope

In scope: read-only identification of references/originals, isolated ordinary translation and automatic layout, per-call accounting, source comparison, generalizable fixes, fresh regression evidence, final native Word review, relevant documentation.

Out of scope: embedding private reference answers in product logic, rewriting old failed runs as passes, marking human review complete automatically, changing saved production preferences without cause, changing external routes/payloads/select values, deleting or replacing drafts, relaxing legal fidelity invariants to obtain green status.

## Tests and acceptance criteria

- All source pages and substantive content accounted for; no omitted/additional legal propositions.
- Names, identifiers, addresses, amounts, dates, fractions and legal references remain faithful to the source and appropriate literal policy.
- Arabic is coherent and correct; acceptable equivalent phrasing need not match the reference byte-for-byte.
- All generated output pages inspected; readable RTL/mixed-direction text, source-supported hierarchy and grouping, no stray generated fields or clipped/overlapping content.
- App succeeds through its normal generation path without manual text/layout correction, retained-response substitution or evaluator bypass.
- Original references, source PDFs, live DB/settings and drafts preserved.
- Provider attempts, failures, usage and unknown charges remain separately recorded.
- Targeted fictional regression coverage and required Standard/Full checks pass with honest qualifications.
- A single successful generation is evidence for that run; repeatability must be measured and any limitations stated.

## Rollout and fallback

All development uses the isolated checkout. Fresh output folders preserve prior attempts. If a run fails, retain it and diagnose before another paid attempt; use offline tests first. Do not rerun unchanged failures indefinitely. Prepare reviewable fixes and tested artifacts before any separately required installation/publication approval.

## Risks and mitigations

- Reference errors: compare against originals, using reference as a reviewed benchmark rather than unquestioned authority.
- Overfitting: general rules, fictional adversarial tests and regression across earlier cases.
- Nondeterministic model output: repeated fresh generation and explicit per-run assessment.
- Cost or transport runaway: bounded existing request policies, visible accounting and finite retries.
- Native automation interference: reuse owned app mechanisms, inspect current Word ownership and preserve user documents.
- Private content disclosure: keep documents and detailed comparisons outside Git; report sanitized defects and counts only.
